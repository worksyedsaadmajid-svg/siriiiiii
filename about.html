About us

An IT services and operational support company built around dependable delivery

Sirius Innovations works with businesses that need both technology capability and operational capacity — combining software development, design, digital marketing and operational support in a single working relationship.

What we do

We provide technology and business services across three connected areas: building software and digital products, growing demand through digital marketing, and running defined business processes on behalf of our clients.

Most of our work begins with a specific problem — a website that no longer represents the business, a process handled manually, a product roadmap larger than the available team, or support volume that has outgrown its capacity. We scope the work, agree how success will be measured, and deliver in reviewable stages.

Depending on what suits you, we work on a fixed project basis, provide a dedicated team that operates inside your process, or take ongoing responsibility for a platform or business function.

Technology delivery

Web, mobile, software and e-commerce development supported by design and QA.

Digital growth

Search, paid media, content and analytics, managed against defined objectives.

Business process operations

Customer support, back-office administration, data operations and technical helpdesk.

How we work

Principles we apply to every engagement

Clear scope before work starts

Every engagement begins with a written understanding of requirements, deliverables and responsibilities.

Technology chosen for fit

We recommend the stack and platform that suit the requirement and your team's ability to maintain it.

Communication as a deliverable

A named contact, an agreed reporting rhythm and visible progress rather than status requests.

Ownership stays with you

Source code, accounts, documentation and process assets are handed over as part of delivery.
